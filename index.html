Road Kings

Role: Lead AI Programmer

A next-generation trucking simulator set in the American South, built on Unreal Engine 5. Published by Focus Entertainment. My responsibility was the entire traffic AI system — from architecture to delivery — supporting hundreds of simultaneous vehicles.

Extended and heavily re-engineered Unreal Engine's CitySample traffic framework to handle AAA simulation-grade demands: robust vehicle awareness, speed management, and cornering that the base sample couldn't support at scale. Leveraged the Mass Entity framework for high-performance entity simulation.

A key architectural decision was designing a unified physics interface shared by player and NPC vehicles — ensuring every physics improvement automatically applied to traffic, eliminating duplication and keeping behaviour consistent throughout development.
